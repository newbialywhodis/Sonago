# Transcription model credits

Sonago downloads these model files from the sherpa-onnx project. Audio is processed locally.

- NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, converted to ONNX and quantized to INT8 by the sherpa-onnx project. [Original model](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- pyannote segmentation 3.0, copyright CNRS, converted to ONNX and quantized to INT8. [Model and MIT license](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0)
- NVIDIA NeMo TitaNet Small speaker embeddings, converted to ONNX. [Sherpa model documentation](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speaker-diarization/pretrained_models.html)
- Silero VAD, Silero Team. [Project and MIT license](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad)
- Zipformer Small audio tagging, trained on AudioSet with Icefall and quantized to INT8. [Model and Apache 2.0 license](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx-zipformer-small-audio-tagging-2024-04-15)
